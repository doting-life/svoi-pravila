# Apology markers (ru/v1). One regex per line.
# Scope: explicit apologies. The adjective «простой» and the noun «прощание» must not match.
# Applied to normalized text (lowercase, ё→е, collapsed space, edge punctuation stripped).
# Every pattern is anchored at a word start.
\bизвини(?:те)?\b
\bпрости(?:те)?\b
\bпрошу прощения\b
\bмне жаль\b
\bсожалею\b
